# Small Business Automation Checklist

Use this checklist to find one practical automation opportunity. Start small: choose a repeated task with a clear result.

## Lead Flow

- [ ] Do inquiries arrive through more than one place, such as email, forms, calls, DMs, or text messages?
- [ ] Are new leads recorded in one clear list?
- [ ] Does every new inquiry receive a timely reply?
- [ ] Are follow-up dates visible?
- [ ] Can you tell which leads are new, active, won, or lost?

## Customer Service

- [ ] Do you answer the same questions repeatedly?
- [ ] Are booking, rescheduling, and delay messages written from scratch each time?
- [ ] Do completed jobs trigger a review request?
- [ ] Are customer handoffs easy to understand?

## Admin

- [ ] Do you copy information between forms, email, and spreadsheets?
- [ ] Do you send the same reminders manually?
- [ ] Do invoices or quote requests require repeated follow-up?
- [ ] Do weekly reports take more than 30 minutes to prepare?

## Content and Sales

- [ ] Do you reuse the same ideas across social posts, email, and proposals?
- [ ] Are proposals or service descriptions recreated every time?
- [ ] Do missed leads disappear without a reminder?

## Pick Your First Automation

Choose one task that:

1. Happens every week.
2. Has a clear trigger.
3. Has a clear next step.
4. Saves time or protects a lead.
5. Can still be reviewed by a person.
