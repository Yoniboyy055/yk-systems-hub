## Beginner Checklist: What Should I Automate First?

Choose a task that scores high on at least three of these:

- It happens every week.
- It takes more than 15 minutes each time.
- It affects leads, sales, service, reporting, or customer experience.
- It follows a predictable pattern.
- It requires copying information between tools.
- It causes mistakes when people are busy.
- It would be valuable even if it only worked 80% of the time with human review.

Do not start with the biggest system. Start with the clearest repeated task.
